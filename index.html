Dragon Offense: Find The Open Spot

The idea: we don't run set plays. We play team basketball. Every Dragon learns the spots on the court, finds the open one, and moves to it. We pass, cut and screen until a teammate is wide open. Then we score. It works against man-to-man and against a zone.

📺 Search YouTube or TikTok: 4 out 1 in motion offenseread and react offense youth basketball

What Dragons Believe On Offense

Everyone Scores

A basket counts the same no matter who makes it. All five Dragons should be ready to score on every trip down the floor.

We Must Pass The Ball

The ball moves faster than any defender can run. Our best shots come after the ball moves, not after one player dribbles.

Off The Ball Matters Most

One Dragon has the ball. Four don't. Those four make the offense work by cutting, screening and moving to open spots.

The 9 Spots

SpotWhere it isHow many
Top spots (T)Outside the 3-point line, just left and right of the middle2
Wing spots (W)Outside the 3-point line on each side, even with the free-throw line2
Free-throw line (FT)The middle of the free-throw line1
Elbows (E)Where the free-throw line meets the lane, on each side2
Blocks (B)The little boxes next to the basket, on each side2

Our starting shape: four Dragons fill the 4 outside spots. One Dragon starts inside, on a block or an elbow. Coaches call this 4 out, 1 in. From there, everyone moves.

How To Find The Open Spot

  • One Dragon per spot. If a teammate is already in the spot you want, go to a different open one.
  • Fill the empty spot. When a teammate cuts away, the closest Dragon slides into the spot they left.
  • Spread out. Stay about 4 big steps (12 to 15 feet) from each other. If you can high-five a teammate, you're too close.
  • Get out of the lane. Never stand in the lane more than 3 seconds. Cut through it, then get out to a spot.
  • Never stand still. Count "1, 2" in your head. If you haven't gotten the ball, move.
  • Give the driver room. When a teammate dribbles toward you, slide away. On a wing, slide down to the corner.

The Four Skills

Skill 1: Passing

Passing is our most important skill. A great pass is just as good as a basket.

PassBest forHow
Chest passQuick, open passesTwo hands, step toward your teammate, thumbs point down at the end.
Bounce passGetting past a tall defender, hitting a cutterBounce it about two-thirds of the way to your teammate.
Overhead passPassing over a defender, skip passesTwo hands over your head, snap it forward.
Pass fakeMoving the defender firstFake one way, then pass the other way.

Passing Rules

  • When you catch it, face the basket and look. That's triple threat: you can shoot, pass or drive.
  • Pass to your teammate's outside hand, away from their defender.
  • Don't hold it. Look, then pass within 2 seconds.
  • Two-dribble rule: no more than 2 dribbles, unless you are driving to the basket or bringing the ball up the court.
  • Everybody touches it. Look for the teammate who hasn't had the ball yet.

Skill 2: Cutting

A cut is a hard, fast run to get open. Walking is not cutting!

CutWhen to use itHow
Basket cut (give-and-go)Right after you passPass, then sprint to the basket with a hand up.
V-cutYour defender is right next to youWalk your defender toward the basket, then sprint back out to a spot. Your path makes a V.
Backdoor cutYour defender jumps in front to block the passPlant your outside foot and sprint behind them to the basket.
Flash cutThe middle is emptySprint to the free-throw line or an elbow with your hands up.
L-cutYou're on the block and want to get to the wingRun up the lane line, then turn out to the wing. Your path makes an L.

No pass after your cut? Get out of the lane and fill an open spot.

Skill 3: Screening

A screen gets a teammate open, and the screener is often open too.

How To Set A Screen

  1. Run to your teammate's defender. Stop and stand still before they bump into you. A moving screen is a foul.
  2. Feet wide, knees bent, arms crossed over your chest.
  3. Call your teammate's name so they know it's coming.

How To Use A Screen

  1. Wait for it! Let your screener get set.
  2. Walk your defender away first, then go.
  3. Run shoulder to shoulder past the screener so nobody fits between you.
  4. Read your defender. They follow you? Curl to the basket. They go under the screen? Pop out for a shot.

After you screen: turn toward the ball and get open. Roll to the basket or step out to an open spot.

Three screens we use: screen away (pass one way, screen the other way), down screen (screen for a teammate near the basket so they can come out), and ball screen (screen for the dribbler).

Skill 4: Moving Without The Ball

Four Dragons don't have the ball. Their job is to get someone open.

  • Pass and move. After every pass, do something: cut to the basket, screen away, or go to a new spot.
  • Fill behind. A teammate cut? Fill their spot.
  • Be a target. Hands up, eyes on the ball, call for it.
  • Drift with the drive. When a teammate drives, slide to an open spot where they can see you.
  • Cut to open space, never to where a teammate already is.

Against A Zone

Same spots, same rules, with a few changes:

  • Stand in the gaps between defenders, not right in front of one.
  • Pass faster to make the zone move. A skip pass (over the top to the far side) finds open shooters.
  • Flash to the free-throw line. It's open against most zones.
  • The inside Dragon runs the baseline, block to block, behind the zone.
  • Dribble even less. Against a zone, the pass is everything.

Dragon Offense Rules

  • Pass the ball. Then pass it again.
  • After you pass, move.
  • Find the open spot. Fill the empty spot.
  • Read your defender, then cut.
  • Screen for a teammate, then get open yourself.
  • Take the open shot you can make.
  • Everybody touches it. Everybody scores.

Practice Games

  • Pass and fill (5 on 0): after every pass, the passer cuts to the basket and everyone fills the empty spots. Ten passes, then a layup.
  • Screen away (4 on 4): every pass is followed by a screen away from the ball.
  • No-dribble scrimmage (5 on 5): no dribbling allowed. You must pass and cut to score.
  • Everybody scores: first team to have all 5 players score wins.

Inbound Plays

These are plays for when we pass the ball in from out of bounds. Two are from under our basket. One is from the sideline. 3 always passes it in.

Coach words: a baseline inbound play is called a BLOB (Baseline Out of Bounds). A sideline inbound play is a SLOB (Sideline Out of Bounds). Use these words when you search for videos.

Inbound Rules

  • 3 yells the play name, then slaps the ball. Nobody moves until the slap!
  • We only have 5 seconds to pass it in. If nobody is open by 4, throw it to the safety.
  • After passing it in, 3 steps onto the court fast.

Inbound 1: BOX

📺 Search YouTube or TikTok: box BLOB inbound play

Start: stand in a box. 4 on the block closest to the ball. 5 on the other block. 2 on the elbow closest to the ball. 1 on the other elbow.

  1. On the slap, 4 walks across the lane and screens 5's defender.
  2. 5 cuts over the screen toward the ball. 5 is the first choice, for a layup.
  3. 2 runs out to the corner. 2 is the second choice, for a shot.
  4. 1 steps back to the top. 1 is the safety.
  5. After screening, 4 turns toward the ball. The screener is often open too!

Inbound 2: LINE

📺 Search YouTube or TikTok: stack BLOB inbound playline inbound play

Start: stand in a line next to the lane: 5 in front (closest to the basket), then 4, then 2, then 1.

  1. On the slap, 5 runs to the basket. First choice.
  2. 4 runs out to the corner. Second choice.
  3. 2 runs across to the other wing.
  4. 1 steps back to the top. Safety.

Why it works: four Dragons going four different ways. Somebody is going to be open!

Inbound 3: Sideline Stack

📺 Search YouTube or TikTok: stack SLOB inbound play

Start: 5 and 4 stand one behind the other near the elbow, 5 in front. 2 in the corner. 1 at the top on the far side. 3 passes it in from the sideline.

  1. On the slap, 4 sprints to the basket. First choice.
  2. 5 steps toward 3 with both hands up. Second choice, the easy pass.
  3. 2 stays in the corner, ready to shoot.
  4. 1 runs back toward half court. Safety: if time is almost up, throw it to 1.

Transition: 3-Lane Fast Break

The idea: as soon as we get the ball, we RUN. If we get down the court before their defense, we get easy layups. Dragons fly!

📺 Search YouTube or TikTok: 3 lane fast breakfill the lanes fast break

Everyone's Job

PlayerJob
5Grab the rebound, turn to the outside, pass to 1. Then jog back as the safety.
1Get open on the side for the outlet pass. Dribble hard down the middle.
2Sprint down the left lane (next to the sideline). Cut to the basket at the end.
3Sprint down the right lane. Cut to the basket at the end.
4Run behind the play as the trailer. Stop at the top of the key.

Steps

  1. 5 rebounds and throws the outlet pass to 1 on the side.
  2. 1 dribbles down the middle of the court. 2 and 3 sprint wide down each sideline.
  3. 1 stops at the free-throw line. 2 and 3 cut to the basket from the sides.
  4. 1 passes to whoever is open, or keeps going for the layup.
  5. 4 arrives as the trailer for a shot or pass. 5 stays back so they can't fast break us.

Fast Break Rules

  • Run wide! Stay in your lane until the free-throw line.
  • Pass ahead. A pass is faster than a dribble.
  • Layups only. If there's no layup, pull it out and run our offense.

Try These Variations

  • 2-ON-1: if only one defender is back, the dribbler drives at them and passes as soon as they commit.
  • 3-ON-2: 1 stops at the free-throw line and passes to the open wing.
  • FOLLOW THE MAKE: after they score, 5 grabs the ball, steps out of bounds fast, and inbounds to 1. We still run!

Press Break: 4-Across

The idea: when the other team presses us, we beat it by passing. Passing is the only way to break a press. A dribbler gets trapped. A pass gets out of the trap. Spread out, get it in, and pass it up the court. Beating a press with passes usually ends in a layup!

📺 Search YouTube or TikTok: 4 across press break1-4 press break

Start: 4 passes it in (tall, good passer). 3, 1, 2 and 5 stand in a line across the free-throw line extended. 3 and 5 on the sidelines, 1 and 2 in the middle.

  1. On the slap, 1 and 2 cut toward the ball to get open.
  2. 3 and 5 sprint long, up the sidelines to half court.
  3. 4 passes to whichever guard is open (usually 1).
  4. 1 turns and looks up the court. First look: pass to 3 up the sideline. Second look: pass to 2 flashing into the middle.
  5. 4 steps in after the pass and stays behind the ball as the safety, in case 1 needs to pass back.

Press Break Rules: Pass, Don't Dribble

  • The only way to beat a press is to pass. No defender can run faster than a pass.
  • Catch, turn, and look up the court. Find the open Dragon before you even think about dribbling.
  • Two defenders coming at you? That means a teammate is open. Pass it!
  • Never dribble into a corner or along the sideline. That's where traps happen.
  • Short, sharp passes to teammates moving toward you. No long lobs.
  • The middle of the court is open. Get the ball there with a pass.
  • No ball? Move to an open spot and give the passer a target.

Try These Variations

  • SCREEN: 2 screens for 1, then cuts to the ball too.
  • LONG: their back defender is too close? 3 or 5 runs all the way to the basket for a pass.
  • RESET: trapped? Pass back to 4, the safety, and swing it to the other side.
  • VS MAN PRESS: same setup. 1 and 2 cut hard to get open, and we pass it up the floor. Never let one player try to dribble through it alone.

Defense 1: Man-To-Man

The idea: you guard one player. Wherever they go, you go.

📺 Search YouTube or TikTok: man to man defense youth basketballhelp side defense

The Golden Rule: Ball – You – Basket. Always stay between your player and the basket. Always be able to see the ball.

Where Do I Stand?

If your player…You should…
Has the ballGet close (one arm away). Stay low. Hand up.
Is one pass awayPut a hand in the passing lane so they can't catch it easily.
Is far from the ballSag toward the middle of the lane. Point one hand at the ball and one at your player.

Guarding The Ball

  1. Get low: feet wide, knees bent, like you're sitting in a chair.
  2. Slide your feet. Don't cross them. Don't jump at fakes.
  3. Push the dribbler toward the sideline, not the middle.
  4. If they stop dribbling, get right up on them and yell "DEAD!"

Helping A Teammate

  • If a teammate gets beat, step in front of the driver and yell "HELP!"
  • Every time the ball is passed, everyone moves. Move while the ball is in the air.

Screens

  • If you see a screen coming, yell "SCREEN!" so your teammate knows.
  • Fight over the screen and stay with your player. Only switch if the coach says "SWITCH."

When They Shoot

  • Yell "SHOT!" Find your player, put your back on them (box out), then go get the ball.
  • Defense isn't done until a Dragon has the ball.

Defense 2: 3-2 Zone

The idea: you guard an area, not a player. Three Dragons stand up top. Two Dragons stand down low by the basket. Whoever comes into your area, you guard them.

We use it when: the other team has good shooters or fast guards.

📺 Search YouTube or TikTok: 3-2 zone defense

Your Job

SpotYour job
Top (1)Pressure the ball. Don't let it go into the middle.
Wings (2, 3)Guard the ball on your side. When the ball is on the other side, slide to the middle.
Low (4, 5)Guard near the basket. Run out to the corner on your side. Rebound!

Where The Ball Goes, We Go

  • Ball at the top: 1 guards it. Everyone else stays in their spot with hands up.
  • Ball on a wing: that wing guards it. 1 slides over to block the pass into the middle.
  • Ball in the corner: the low player on that side runs out to guard it. The other low player slides over to the block.
  • Ball in the middle (free-throw line): the closest player jumps on it right away.

Watch out! The 3-2 has holes in the middle and down by the baseline. Talk a lot so nobody gets lost.

Remember

  • Hands up and wide. Make it hard to pass.
  • Move while the pass is in the air, not after it's caught.
  • When they shoot, find someone near you and box out.

Defense 3: 1-3-1 Zone

The idea: one Dragon up top, three Dragons in a line across the middle, and one Dragon down on the baseline. It looks like a plus sign.

We use it when: we want to trap the ball and get steals.

📺 Search YouTube or TikTok: 1-3-1 zone defense1-3-1 trap

Your Job

SpotYour job
Top (1)Chase the ball to one side. Then don't let them pass it back to the top.
Wings (2, 3)Trap the ball with 1 when it comes to your side. Long arms!
Middle (5)Stand at the free-throw line. Don't let anyone catch it there.
Baseline (4)Run corner to corner. Guard whoever has the ball down low.

What's a trap? Two Dragons guard the player with the ball at the same time. Box them in with your feet and keep your hands straight up. No reaching, no fouling.

Where The Ball Goes, We Go

  • Ball at the top: 1 guards it and pushes it to one side.
  • Ball on a wing: that wing and 1 trap it. Everyone else watches for a bad pass to steal.
  • Ball in the corner: 4 sprints out and guards it. 5 drops down to the block.
  • Ball goes to the other side: everyone slides over and does the same thing on that side.

Watch out! The corners are the weak spot. 4 has to run hard and yell "CORNER!" every time.

Remember

  • Traps use feet, not hands.
  • After a trap, look for the steal.
  • When they shoot, everyone crashes the boards.

Full Court Press: 2-2-1

The idea: we play defense on the whole court. We let them pass it in, then trap them by the sideline and steal it. Breathe fire!

📺 Search YouTube or TikTok: 2-2-1 press2-2-1 zone press

Where We Start (After We Score)

RowWhoJob
Front1 and 2Stand at the free-throw line. Let the pass come in, then push the dribbler to the sideline.
Middle3 and 4Stand near half court. When the ball comes up your sideline, run up and trap.
Back5Stay back by our basket. Steal long passes. Never let anyone behind you.

Steps

  1. They pass it in. The front player on that side gets in front of the dribbler and keeps them on the sideline. Don't let them go to the middle!
  2. When the dribbler gets near half court, the middle player on that side runs up and traps with the front player.
  3. The other front player sprints to the middle to steal the pass back.
  4. The other middle player slides to the middle of the court.
  5. 5 moves toward the ball side to steal a long pass.

Press Rules

  • Trap with your feet and chest. Hands up. No reaching, no fouling.
  • Keep the ball out of the middle.
  • If they get past the press, everyone sprints back to our basket.

Try These Variations

  • HALF COURT TRAP: the front players start at half court instead. Less running, still traps.
  • DENY: the front players guard the inbound passer's teammates closely to try to get a 5-second call.
  • FAKE PRESS: line up in the press, then drop back to our half-court defense. It wastes their time.
